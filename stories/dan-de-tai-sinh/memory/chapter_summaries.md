# Chapter Summaries

> Compact finalized-event log. Add only after each final chapter exists.

## Genesis / Chapter 0 Handoff

Opening reality: Tạ Trường An’s consciousness stabilized in a 17-year-old injured Thanh Huyền outer disciple body at Luyện Khí 3, with 20 days before assessment, 7 low-grade spirit stones, no furnace/spirit fire and a suspicious Hồi Khí Tán packet whose residue only resembles an old processing signature.

## Chapter 1 — Lô Thuốc Số Mười Bảy

Tạ Trường An rejects the impure Dẫn Khí Đan and high-level treatments his current body cannot support, then devises a low-rank external three-herb method for stabilizing inflamed meridians. At Outer Medicine Hall he learns from Cố Tùng Niên that his Hồi Khí Tán is **lot 17**, imported via **Cửu Phương Nam kho** seven days earlier. He pays 1 spirit stone and accepts a three-morning herb-sorting bargain for treatment materials and ordinary medicine-stove access; during the first session he correctly distinguishes damp herbs from a separate over-smoked/insect-contaminated subset, earning only cautious professional attention from Cố. The first treatment slightly reduces acute inflammation but does not heal the damaged Hand Shaoyang segment or raise cultivation. End state: 6 spirit stones, 2 impure Dẫn Khí Đan unused, lot-17 packet retained, two work/treatment sessions still owed, no furnace, and the Cửu Phương lead explicitly remains unproven.
