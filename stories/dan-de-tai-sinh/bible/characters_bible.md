# 2. Character Baselines

## MC001 — Tạ Trường An

### Identity

- **Gender:** nam
- **Age:** 17 in current body; memories of a life that reached several centuries.
- **Origin:** current body from declining Tạ family of Lạc Hà; previous life was a renowned alchemist in upper regions.
- **Social status:** ordinary outer disciple at opening.
- **Faction:** Thanh Huyền Tông initially.
- **Cultivation path:** repaired foundation through Thanh Mạch Quy Nguyên Pháp + alchemy-assisted but resource-limited progression.
- **Starting realm:** Luyện Khí tầng 3, meridians injured.
- **Previous-life peak:** Luyện Hư viên mãn; Alchemy Emperor title reflects craft, not invincible combat.

### Visual Anchors

- Lean body not yet fully recovered; fingers steady when handling medicine despite weakness.
- Quiet gaze that often pauses on material details before people notice what he is measuring.
- Outer disciple robe repaired at cuff; no luxurious artifact.

### Core Baseline

- **Core desire:** regain agency, rebuild cultivation and uncover who engineered his death.
- **Deeper need:** learn that sharing risk and trusting capable people is not the same as losing control.
- **Core fear:** becoming dependent and helpless at the decisive moment again.
- **Formative wound:** betrayal during breakthrough when his protective formation failed from inside.
- **Non-negotiable value:** he will not casually sacrifice uninvolved people for personal advancement.
- **Blind spot:** overvalues his own ability to absorb uncertainty alone.
- **Internal contradiction:** wants allies yet instinctively withholds enough information to prevent them from acting as equals.
- **Social mask:** calm, practical, slightly aloof outer disciple.
- **Private tendency:** replays failure conditions and seeks hidden dependencies.

### Decision Tendencies

- Approach: identify constraints, find cheapest test, keep one reserve if possible.
- Risk tolerance: high when expected value is clear, low for vanity fights.
- Planning horizon: long, but can over-plan from outdated memories.
- Preferred leverage: information, preparation, resource asymmetry.
- Overvalues: private control and secrecy.
- Undervalues: how much allies can improve a plan when given context.
- Mistake pattern: assumes an old pattern still holds, then commits too late to updating because his prior model once worked.

### Voice Anchors

- Sentence length: usually short–medium, few wasted words.
- Directness: high with peers, formal enough with elders.
- Humor: dry, rare.
- Avoids saying directly: fear of betrayal; how much he needs other people.
- Original sample lines:
  - “Ta không cần thứ tốt nhất. Ta cần thứ dùng được trước khi trời sáng.”
  - “Nếu hắn muốn ta tin tin tức này, vậy trước hết xem hắn đã bỏ bao nhiêu vốn để khiến ta tin.”
  - “Đan phương không sai. Người luyện mới là kẻ đang ép nó làm việc vượt quá cái lò này chịu nổi.”

### Cultivation / Combat Identity

- Cultivates to regain freedom and test a better path than previous life.
- Preferred style early: evade, read, poison/medicine knowledge, low-cost talisman/pháp khí, terrain.
- Tactical strength: preparation and resource accounting.
- Tactical weakness: low raw power/body endurance; old instincts may demand capabilities the current body lacks.
- Killing: pragmatic in true life-or-death conflict, not casual.
- Breakthrough psychology: distrusts “just push through”; checks failure modes obsessively after past death.

### Hard Contradiction Boundaries

1. Cannot suddenly trust another person with full rebirth secret without sustained relationship change.
2. Cannot casually duel a Trúc Cơ cultivator head-on at Luyện Khí.
3. Cannot produce high-rank pills without matching soul, fire, cauldron and materials.

### Long Arc Direction

From solitary controller → selective collaborator → leader who shares responsibility and builds a transparent, resilient alchemy institution with safeguards.

## FL001 — Lục Thanh Nghi

### Identity

- **Gender:** nữ
- **Age:** 18
- **Origin:** Lục family once maintained border formations; family lost status after a failed mine-sealing incident.
- **Status:** elite outer disciple attached to Formation Hall.
- **Faction:** Thanh Huyền Tông.
- **Cultivation path:** water-metal aligned formation technique, uses compact formation nails and a short sword as anchor tool.
- **Starting realm:** Luyện Khí tầng 8.

### Visual Anchors

- Dark blue practical robe with narrow sleeves; small copper formation nails stored in a waist tube.
- Looks at rooms in lines and angles before faces.
- Habitually taps one fingertip twice when checking a formation count.

### Core Baseline

- **Core desire:** find the truth behind her elder brother Lục Duy Phong’s disappearance two years ago.
- **Deeper need:** accept that control and proof are not the only forms of safety.
- **Core fear:** accusing the wrong person and destroying what remains of her family.
- **Formative wound:** her family was blamed for a formation failure with incomplete evidence.
- **Non-negotiable value:** does not forge evidence even against someone she hates.
- **Blind spot:** can keep investigating after the human cost exceeds the value of one more piece of proof.
- **Internal contradiction:** distrusts secrecy yet keeps her own investigation secret.
- **Social mask:** cool, precise, self-contained.
- **Private tendency:** preserves tiny physical clues others discard.

### Decision Tendencies

- Approach: map structure, identify missing data, create controlled test.
- Risk tolerance: medium; rises sharply when a clue concerns her brother.
- Planning horizon: medium-long.
- Preferred leverage: terrain, formations, evidence.
- Overvalues: provability.
- Undervalues: intuition and emotional testimony.
- Mistake pattern: delays acting while seeking confirmation.

### Voice Anchors

- Precise, low ornament, often frames claims by confidence level.
- Sample lines:
  - “Ta nói nó đáng ngờ, không nói nó là chứng cứ.”
  - “Ngươi muốn đi qua thì được. Đừng giẫm lên viên đá thứ ba.”
  - “Nếu phải chọn giữa đúng và thắng, ít nhất nói rõ ngươi đang chọn cái nào.”

### Relationship Baselines

| Target | Trust | Respect | Affection | Fear | Resentment | Obligation | Public mask |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Tạ Trường An | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | chưa quen |
| Formation Hall | 2 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 3 | disciple loyalty |
| Lục Duy Phong | 4 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 0 | 5 | missing brother |

### Cultivation / Combat Identity

- Cultivates to regain ability to protect family without depending on reputation.
- Preferred range: mid-range control with micro-formations; short sword only when space collapses.
- Strength: terrain conversion, trap recognition, team coordination.
- Weakness: needs setup/materials; direct burst weaker than sword prodigies.
- Artifacts: treats them as tools, not status.
- Killing: reluctant unless threat is clear and immediate.

### Secrets

| Secret | Who knows initially | Why hidden | Reveal condition | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| investigating brother’s disappearance | herself | fears suppression | trust grows / evidence requires help | joins MC resource mystery |
| keeps fragment of brother’s broken formation plate | herself | only surviving clue | secret realm/ledger match | ties disappearance to trade chain |

### Hard Contradiction Boundaries

1. Cannot become romantically devoted after one rescue or one impressive feat.
2. Cannot abandon evidence standards merely because she trusts MC.
3. Cannot be reduced to support alchemist; her formations must independently alter outcomes.

### Long Arc Direction

Rival/parallel investigator → trusted tactical partner → equal who can challenge MC’s secrecy → romantic partner and co-founder of a new institution. Remains precise and independently motivated.

## MEN001 — Cố Tùng Niên

### Identity

- Nam, 61; outer Medicine Hall steward; Trúc Cơ hậu kỳ; bậc-2 đan sư từng thất bại khi thi bậc 3.
- Former inner disciple who chose administrative stability after a damaged soul-fire meridian.

### Core Baseline

- Desire: keep Medicine Hall functioning without letting talented juniors be consumed by faction games.
- Fear: betting on another genius who burns out or drags others down.
- Value: craft claims must survive a furnace, not reputation.
- Blind spot: cynicism makes him test sincerity too long.
- Mask: grumbling accountant.
- Private: carefully tracks which poor disciples actually repay small favors.

### Decision Tendencies

Tests people with small responsibilities before large trust; low appetite for political risk; respects reproducible technique.

### Voice Anchors

- “Đừng gọi ta tiền bối khi ngươi còn nợ Dược Đường ba linh thạch.”
- “Lò không biết nịnh người. Hỏng là hỏng.”

### Cultivation / Combat

Defensive fire/wood techniques; poor in prolonged battle due old injury. Strong in practical herb appraisal.

### Hard Contradictions

1. Does not worship MC after one demonstration.
2. Will not sacrifice Medicine Hall juniors to protect sect face.
3. Will not knowingly help Tịch Đăng if he discovers its coercive structure.

## ANT001 — Triệu Kính

### Identity

- Nam, 43; deputy deacon handling outer medicine allocation; Trúc Cơ trung kỳ.
- Publicly efficient and polite; privately profits through a Cửu Phương sub-agent connected to a Tịch Đăng cell.
- He does **not** know the full organization.

### Core Baseline

- Desire: accumulate enough capital/status to move family into inner-city safety.
- Fear: losing position and having his dependents dragged down with him.
- Value: obligations are transactional but real once paid.
- Blind spot: believes everyone has a price if enough pressure is applied.
- Contradiction: dislikes cruelty yet sustains a system that uses it at arm’s length.

### Decision Tendencies

- Collect data before confrontation.
- Uses cheap probes first: stock changes, assignments, rumors, low-level intermediaries.
- Retreats when risk exceeds payoff.
- Mistake pattern: interprets principled refusal as hidden bidding rather than genuine boundary.

### Voice Anchors

- “Không ai cấm ngươi chọn. Ta chỉ nói rõ mỗi lựa chọn giá bao nhiêu.”
- “Tin đồn rẻ. Phản ứng của người nghe tin đồn mới đáng tiền.”

### Hard Contradictions

1. Does not personally attack MC merely because offended.
2. Does not know Tịch Đăng leadership.
3. Will preserve evidence useful as leverage rather than destroy everything reflexively.

## COREANT001 — Lương Tĩnh Viễn

### Identity

- Nam; apparent age late 50s, true age several centuries.
- Hợp Đạo trung kỳ at story opening.
- Former scholar involved in post-Nam Thiên disaster research; secret strategic leader of Tịch Đăng Hội.

### Core Baseline

- **Core desire:** prevent uncontrolled high-realm breakthroughs from causing another realm-scale collapse.
- **Deeper need he refuses:** accept that control concentrated in one network creates its own catastrophe.
- **Core fear:** mass access to half-understood breakthrough methods.
- **Formative wound:** survived the Vỡ Linh Triều Nam Thiên and lost an entire research cohort.
- **Non-negotiable value:** data about systemic risk must be preserved even if individuals are sacrificed.
- **Blind spot:** treats people as variables once stakes become large enough.
- **Internal contradiction:** claims to preserve civilization while using coercion, assassination and engineered scarcity.
- **Social mask:** mild scholar, patient listener.

### Decision Tendencies

- Planning horizon: decades.
- Preferred leverage: supply chains, information asymmetry, institutional dependency.
- Creates competing hypotheses and tests them cheaply.
- Avoids dramatic personal intervention if a cell can solve the problem.
- Mistake pattern: underestimates trust networks because they are difficult to model quantitatively.

### Voice Anchors

- “Một phương pháp đúng trong tay một người chưa chắc đúng khi vạn người cùng dùng.”
- “Ta không cần ngươi đồng ý. Ta cần ngươi tính hết hậu quả trước khi gọi nó là tự do.”
- “Nếu giả thuyết sai, bỏ nó. Đừng bảo vệ lòng tự trọng bằng thêm một trăm mạng người.”

### Secrets

- Authorized the containment/assassination operation against previous-life Tạ Trường An after negotiation channels failed.
- Does not know MC reincarnated.
- Possesses incomplete notes about Quy Nhất Vấn Đạo Đan and believes the missing principle died with Tạ Trường An.

### Hard Contradiction Boundaries

1. Does not send top-tier assassins at an unexplained outer disciple without evidence.
2. Does not cling to a disproved hypothesis for pride.
3. Does not reveal the full Tịch Đăng network in monologue.
4. Final defeat must require breaking his information/resource structure, not merely overpowering him in one duel.

### Long Arc Direction

Remote system pressure → intellectual/strategic opponent → personal conflict over who may control dangerous knowledge → defeat through loss of network legitimacy, information monopoly and final combat. His motivation remains intelligible even when methods become indefensible.
