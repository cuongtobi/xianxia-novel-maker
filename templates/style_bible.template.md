- Bootstrap retirement condition: project examples cover required prose functions

# 4. Originality Contract

Examples may calibrate:

- rhythm;
- narrative distance;
- dialogue texture;
- descriptive density;
- action/exposition balance;
- paragraph movement;
- degree of explicitness.

Examples may **not** be copied for:

- wording;
- distinctive imagery/metaphor;
- rhetorical frames;
- paragraph sequence;
- scene structure;
- plot beats;
- character/world identity.

# 5. Prose Edit Boundary

The editor should prefer leaving good prose untouched.

Permitted local improvements:

- remove explanation already demonstrated by scene;
- compress report-like reasoning;
- repair unnatural Vietnamese / convert-like clause order;
- trim obvious repetition;
- improve local clarity.

Do not standardize cadence or paragraph length. Do not manufacture fragments, aphorisms or punchlines.
